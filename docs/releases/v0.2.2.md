CAVY 0.2.2: teaching allotments, lab start times and a tidier "My class".

- **Teaching (admin panel).** Subjects per programme and semester (the MSc Data Science ones are built in). An administrator allots a subject, a division and a batch (or both batches) to a professor. A new Academic year card shows who sits in which year.
- **Access follows the allotments (classroom server).** A professor sees only the students allotted to them, with a count per allotment, and can aim labs only at the course, year, division and batch allotted to them. With nothing allotted, everything is switched off with a "waiting for your assignment" notice until an administrator allots them something. Professors no longer pick their own courses or add students to a class on a server.
- **My class (professor).** Search and Course / Division / Batch filters (including both batches) over a compact scrolling table, in the app's own style.
- **Labs.** An optional start time; "any batch" labs can be made once per batch with their own times; Duplicate copies a lab to another division, batch or time. Students see only labs from professors who teach them.
- **Problem and instructions.** A lab has a problem statement and separate instructions. Students see neither (nor the difficulty) until the assessment stage starts; they see the title and topic. Both are written in Markdown, rendered in a fixed-size scrolling panel. "Draft with AI" uses one saved prompt that produces both parts.
